## 3. Projections, fallbacks and V2/V3 paths

- **V3 projection:** `families/{f}/gamification_summaries/{memberId}` — authoritative
  `xpTotal`, `level`, `xpProgressInLevel`, `xpToNextLevel`, `bestStreak`,
  `currentStreak`. Consumed by `gamificationAdapters.ts`, `ChildSummaryCard.tsx`,
  `MemberProfile.tsx`, `Dashboard.tsx`, `Rewards.tsx`, `Family.tsx`, `achievements.ts`.
- **Legacy fallback (V1/V2):** `users/{id}.rewardPoints`, `.lifetimeXP`,
  `.currentStreak`, `.longestStreak` — used as a fallback source in
  `gamificationAdapters.ts` (e.g. `rawXp = projectionUsable ? summary.xpTotal : member.lifetimeXP`).
- **V2/V3 ledger:** `families/{f}/gamification_events` (XP event ledger),
  `task_occurrences` (task completion dedupe), `behaviour_events` (behaviour intent
  log), `reversals` (V2 reversal records).
